A molotov cocktail for DayZ, made from things you already find: a glass bottle, some petrol and a rag. Light it, throw it, and whatever it hits burns.

[h2]How it works[/h2]
[list]
[*] Make one: fill a glass bottle with at least 200 ml of petrol or diesel (from a jerry can), then combine it with a rag in your inventory. One rag from the stack goes into the neck.
[*] Light it: hold the molotov with a lighter or matches anywhere in your inventory and choose [b]Light the rag[/b]. Lighting it with a lighter in your hands while the molotov lies on the ground works too.
[*] Throw it. A lit molotov bursts on whatever it hits - ground, wall, car or person - into a pool of fire about 3.5 m across that burns for 20 seconds. Shooting a lit one bursts it too.
[*] Don't hang on to it: 30 seconds after lighting it bursts on its own, at your feet if you're still holding it. A lit molotov can't be put away in your inventory.
[*] The fire burns anyone standing in it, and clothing doesn't help: players lose about 10 health a second (plus shock), infected and animals 25 a second. Running straight through costs a little; standing in it is deadly.
[*] Fuel goes up: a car with fuel in the tank caught in the fire explodes a few seconds later, like a fuel pump does, and is wrecked. Molotov a fuel pump and it explodes too. Keep 5 m back - the blast hurts. What's left burns for a minute and can set off the next fuelled car parked beside it.
[*] An empty car caught in the fire only loses engine and fuel tank health; left in it for the whole burn it ends up with a ruined engine.
[*] An unlit molotov is just a heavy bottle: drop it or throw it and nothing happens.
[*] Our own model (a vodka bottle with a petrol-soaked rag knotted round the neck, half full of fuel), built from vanilla textures.
[/list]

[h2]For server owners[/h2]
[b]Install[/b]
Paths are relative to the server's install folder (the one with DayZServer_x64.exe or DayZServer), shown here as DayZServer/.
[olist]
[*] Put the mod's folder in the server's install folder as DayZServer/@Molotov (with its subfolders addons/, keys/ and extras/).
[*] Add it to the server's launch parameter, after your other mods: -mod=...;@Molotov
[*] Copy the key file DayZServer/@Molotov/keys/tarbaby.bikey into DayZServer/keys/ (next to dayz.bikey). Without it, players using the mod are kicked for unsigned files. If you already run a mod signed with tarbaby.bikey (the Hand Truck mods are), you have it already.
[*] Copy the loot entry DayZServer/@Molotov/extras/types_molotov.xml into a folder of your mission, for example DayZServer/mpmissions/dayzOffline.chernarusplus/molotov/types_molotov.xml (or into a folder you already use for modded types).
[*] Register that file in your mission's DayZServer/mpmissions/dayzOffline.chernarusplus/cfgeconomycore.xml, inside <economycore>, using the folder name from step 4:
[code]
<ce folder='molotov'>
    <file name='types_molotov.xml' type='types' />
</ce>
[/code]
If you used a folder that already has a <ce> block, add just the <file ... /> line to it.
[*] Restart the server.
[/olist]
Players only need to subscribe; the DayZ launcher loads the mod for them.

Molotovs never spawn as loot: the entry only lets the economy keep crafted ones (like the vanilla torch, 4 hours on the ground). Instead of steps 4 and 5 you can paste the <type> entry from types_molotov.xml into your mission's db/types.xml. The bottles, petrol and rags are all vanilla loot, so nothing else is needed.
