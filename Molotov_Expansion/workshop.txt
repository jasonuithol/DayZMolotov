[b]This add-on has moved:[/b] DayZ Expansion's vehicle wrecks are now in [b]Better Explosions - Expansion Vehicles[/b], an add-on for the Better Explosions mod (which Molotov 0.6 and later requires). This item is kept so servers that list it keep working: it has nothing of its own and just requires the new add-on.

New servers: use Better Explosions - Expansion Vehicles instead. Servers already running this one can swap it in their -mod line for @BetterExplosions_Expansion, or keep it and add @BetterExplosions_Expansion before it:
-mod=...;@BetterExplosions;@BetterExplosions_Expansion;@Molotov;@Molotov_Expansion

What it gives you, through the new add-on: when a fuelled Expansion vehicle goes up, it's swapped for the matching vanilla wreck (tractor, Ikarus bus, UAZ, Land Rover, crashed Huey), burnt wheels, doors, bonnets and rotor blades fly off, and its cargo is thrown out around it. Wrecks are gone at the next restart.

Requires: Molotov (0.6 or later), Better Explosions - Expansion Vehicles, DayZ-Expansion-Vehicles.
