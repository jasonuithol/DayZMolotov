# Changelog

## 0.2 - 2026-11-13

Everything this add-on did moved to Better Explosions - Expansion Vehicles
(BetterExplosions_Expansion), which it now requires; it has nothing of its own left. You
can replace it with that add-on in -mod, or keep it: either way the Expansion vehicles
leave their wrecks as before.

## 0.1 - 2026-11-10

First release. DayZ Expansion vehicles that go up in a molotov fire leave the matching
vanilla wreck, like the vanilla cars do:

- Tractor: the tractor wreck
- Bus: the Ikarus bus wreck
- UAZ (every variant): the UAZ wreck
- Land Rover: Nasdara's Land Rover wreck
- UH-1H helicopter: the crashed Huey

Burnt pieces fly off each one, as off the vanilla cars:

- Tractor: both burnt wheels and a door
- Bus: three burnt wheels, both sets of doors and the engine cover
- UAZ and Land Rover: two burnt wheels, the bonnet and a door
- UH-1H: two burnt rotor blades and both cabin doors

What was in its cargo is thrown out on the ground round the wreck. Wrecks and flying bits
are gone at restart. Server and client; needs Molotov 0.5.
